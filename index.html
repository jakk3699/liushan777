Transaction safety

Understand the payment terms before you proceed

Some partner platforms may require identity checks or a linked bank account. Review the official platform instructions and all terms before approving anything.

Protect your account and money

  • Complete payment steps only on the official platform or app.
  • Never share your password or one-time verification code.
  • Do not transfer money or top up an account to unlock a payout.
  • Ask for clear details of any authorization, fee or payout restriction.

FAQ

Good questions

How do you decide what to pay?

We consider the item type, brand, model, age, condition, quantity and current resale or recycling value. Send clear photos and details for a free initial estimate; the final offer and any conditions will be explained before you decide.

How and when do I get paid?

The payment method and timing depend on the agreed transaction and any partner platform involved. We’ll explain the terms before you confirm.

What can't you take?

Hazardous waste such as chemicals, paint and gas cylinders, and anything wet, mouldy or infested. Not sure about something? Send a photo and ask.

What about the data on my old phone or laptop?

Back up your files and remove personal accounts before handover. If possible, reset the device to factory settings. Ask us about any data-handling steps before confirming collection.

How does the community donation rate work?

The stated 20% is based on the platform partnership rebate actually received. It is not a deduction from the amount agreed with you for your items. Actual rebate receipts, donation amounts and records should be explained clearly.
